FUEL RIGHT

NUTRITION GUIDE

📈 MUSCLE GAIN

  • 01 Eat in a slight caloric surplus — about 300–500 calories above maintenance
  • 02 Prioritize protein: aim for 1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight daily
  • 03 Don't fear carbs — they fuel your workouts and recovery
  • 04 Eat whole foods 80% of the time: lean meats, rice, oats, veggies, fruits
  • 05 Stay hydrated — aim for at least 2.5 liters of water daily

🔥 FAT LOSS

  • 01 Create a moderate deficit — about 300–500 calories below maintenance
  • 02 Keep protein high to preserve muscle while losing fat
  • 03 Focus on volume eating: big salads, lean proteins, fiber-rich foods
  • 04 Add daily walks — 8,000–10,000 steps burns significant calories
  • 05 Sleep 7–9 hours — poor sleep sabotages fat loss hormones

MINDSET

STAY MOTIVATED

Fitness isn't just about the body. It's about building a mindset that carries you through life.

🎯

Set Small Goals

Don't aim for perfection. Aim for one more rep, one healthier meal, or 10 more minutes of sleep. Small wins compound into massive change.

⚡

Show Up on Bad Days

The workouts you do when you don't feel like it are the ones that define you. Even 20 minutes counts. Consistency beats intensity.

✨

Celebrate Progress

Take photos, log your weights, track your energy levels. Looking back at where you started is the most powerful motivator.
